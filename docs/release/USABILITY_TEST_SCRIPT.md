# Moderated usability test script

Status: **Prepared; participants have not yet been run**

Do not report usability testing as complete until real participants perform this
script and consent to the recorded observations.

## Participants

Recruit at least one person in each role:

1. Compliance or GRC manager familiar with SOC 2
2. Security or engineering partner who can confirm technical scope and evidence
3. Founder, operations leader, general counsel, or federal go-to-market leader

Do not coach FedRAMP terminology unless the participant asks. The product should
make the next decision understandable.

## Tasks

1. Determine whether the fictional company may fit Class A.
2. Explain which answers caused the result and what it does not guarantee.
3. Identify a scope concern involving a carved-out provider.
4. Import or enter a SOC 2 control and explain the extraction/import confidence.
5. Review a deterministic mapping suggestion and explain why it appeared.
6. Reject one suggestion and confirm one as partial support.
7. Explain what the mapped SOC 2 control establishes and what remains missing.
8. Add an evidence candidate and identify its freshness/review state.
9. Complete one Key Security Indicator workflow and create a technical follow-up.
10. Find the highest-priority next action.
11. Generate the SOC 2 Reuse and Gap Report and explain its principal limitation.
12. Export the project, delete local data, and explain how it can be restored.
13. Find the Ancilis readiness-review option and explain what is sent when clicked.
14. Explain where project data is stored and what ordinary network activity occurs.
15. Explain what Class A Bridge does not certify, approve, assess, or submit.

## Observation record

For every task record:

- Completion: independent, completed with hint, or not completed
- Time on task
- Errors and recovery
- Questions asked
- Terminology or content that caused confusion
- Confidence before and after the task
- Accessibility or input-method issue
- Participant recommendation
- Severity: release blocker, high, medium, low, or observation

## Release decision

Resolve release-blocking findings before 1.0.0. If participants are unavailable,
retain the public-beta designation and record this study as pending; never invent
completion, quotes, confidence scores, or approval.
