# Customer value validation

## Primary outcome

A compliance lead with a SOC 2 Type II starting point can explain their Class A preparation concerns and leave with a useful plan to discuss with colleagues, without an account or uploading confidential documents to a server.

## Study protocol (not yet run)

Recruit five representative users, including compliance, engineering and a federal business owner. Obtain consent before recording observations. Use fictional or customer-approved redacted material; do not collect report contents through telemetry.

Ask participants to start without coaching, complete the eight-question interview, explain their result, download the starting plan, identify who owns the next task, import a control, review one candidate, and save/restore a portable backup.

Record elapsed time to useful exported plan, independent task completion, hints, errors, misunderstandings and corrections. Ask the participant to explain the uncertainty and certification boundary in their own words.

Proposed gate: at least four of five users obtain a useful plan within five minutes without coaching, and every participant understands that the result does not confer certification. Investigate every material misunderstanding before wider release. These thresholds are proposed product gates, not statistical population claims.

## Qualified case review (pending)

Have qualified reviewers independently label at least twenty representative cases before viewing the tool's outputs. Cover current/stale assessments, uncertain dates, different offerings, material federal differences, carve-outs with and without reviewed coverage, exceptions with and without remediation, non-cloud offerings, missing use cases, and other eligible frameworks.

Record source version, rationale, expected classification, material blockers and appropriate actions. Compare the tool's result and exported plan to the adjudicated reference. Any critical false reassurance blocks release claims until corrected. Track false escalation as well as missed concerns.

## Reuse effectiveness (pending)

Use equivalent control sets and counterbalance whether each reviewer starts manually or with the tool. Measure total review time including corrections, accepted useful candidates, irrelevant candidates, omitted material relationships, and completeness of the final handoff. Demonstrate reduced effort without increased material omissions before claiming time savings.

## Operational checks

Fresh automation covers output escaping, catalog compatibility, starting actions, empty states, schema validation, browser journeys, privacy canaries and backup lifecycle. A production release operator must separately verify HTTPS/headers, root and app routes, offline restart, updates, downloads and restore on actual supported hardware. Record the deployed commit and URL.

No usage telemetry is added to the assessment workspace. Research observations are opt-in and separate from confidential project data.
